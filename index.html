Our Mission

Lift Up Minds, Empower Neighborhoods

The LUMEN Initiative is a student-led nonprofit working to expand access to education and spark curiosity in young people. We believe learning should reach every student, whatever their background or resources. So we bring it through interactive workshops, real experiments, and community events — partnering with local schools, libraries, universities, hospitals, and other community centers.

LUMEN Initiative is formed exclusively for charitable, educational, scientific, and literary purposes within the scope of 501(c)(3) organizations with no private inurement.

Our Inspiration

“Hiding the vegetables”

Mark Rober calls it “hiding the vegetables”: you can’t teach kids who aren’t paying attention, so you tuck the learning inside something they can’t look away from. He bet $60 million on that idea — glitter bombs, squirrel obstacle courses, elephant toothpaste. We're betting on the same idea at LUMEN: the science hides in the eruption, the math in the escape room. And we have more than science.

I can’t teach you if I don’t have your attention. But if I can get your attention with something remarkable, well, now I have something to attach the learning to.

Mark Rober, “My $60 Million Science Experiment,” TED 2026

Astronomy

Unveiling the Universe 101

Travel from the Big Bang to a black hole’s event horizon — through cosmic inflation, the microwave background, the lives of stars, and the galaxies they build. Then design a star or planet of your own.

Biology

DNA From a Strawberry

Crush, soap, salt, ice-cold alcohol — and one strawberry. By the end of class you’ll be holding real DNA in your hand and tracing it back to cells, genes, and the proteins they build.

Computer Science

Build the Web, Block by Block

HTML is a stack of building blocks — and you’re the architect. Combine a hero, an about, a showcase, and an interactive section across 625+ design combinations, then live-code your page right in the browser.

Chemistry

Elephant Toothpaste & the Power of Catalysts

Hydrogen peroxide, yeast, soap, color — and a foamy eruption you’ll feel the heat of. Watch a catalyst do its job in real time and trace it back to atoms, molecules, and how fast they collide.

Engineering

Mini Payload Lander Challenge

Your probe has 60 minutes to land safely on Titan. With straws, balloons, and bubble wrap, your team prototypes a crash-resistant lander for a ping-pong-ball payload — and learns first-hand how engineers trade off energy dissipation, air resistance, and material limits.

Mathematics

Mystery Escape Room

Four rooms, four geometry puzzles, one 4-digit vault code. Through the Area Room, Perimeter Passage, Hidden Floor Plan, and Shape Decoder, you’ll crack a narrative built around the math you already know — used in ways you haven’t seen.

Physics

Let’s Make Oobleck

Mix cornstarch and water — get a fluid that’s solid when you hit it and liquid when you don’t. Press, punch, scoop, and squeeze your way into the strange world of non-Newtonian viscosity, and take home a stress ball you made yourself.

Economics

Owning a Slice of the Lemonade Stand

A lemonade stand has shareholders, profit, and risk too — just at smaller scale. In 45 minutes we’ll cover ownership, supply and demand, and how prices move, then turn loose a no-risk trading sim where you’ll buy, sell, and watch your portfolio swing.

Courses

Eight workshops. Come to one, or come to all of them.

Every course is built and taught by students, runs in an hour or less, and stands on its own — there is no sequence to keep up with and nothing to catch up on.

Astronomy Unveiling the Universe 101 Start at the Big Bang, end up designing a star of your own. Talk & design lab → Biology DNA From a Strawberry Soap, salt and cold alcohol pull real DNA out of fruit — you can see it. Hands-on lab → Computer Science Build the Web, Block by Block Four sections, five options each — 625 possible pages, and you write one. Live coding → Chemistry Elephant Toothpaste Set off a foam eruption, then work out what the catalyst actually did. Demo & discussion → Engineering Mini Payload Lander Challenge Land a probe on Titan with straws and tape without cracking the payload. Team design challenge → Mathematics Mystery Escape Room Four geometry puzzles. Each one hands you a digit of the vault code. Puzzle hunt → Physics Let’s Make Oobleck Punch a liquid and watch it turn solid. Take the stress ball home. For parents

The practical answers, before you have to ask.

Who it’s for
Middle schoolers, roughly grades 5–8.
What it costs
Nothing, ever. LUMEN is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit and we supply the materials.
Where
Public libraries around Mercer County, New Jersey — Princeton, Plainsboro, Hightstown, Hickory Corner and Robbinsville.
How to join
Register on the hosting library’s events calendar — here’s how. No application, no interview, no prior experience.
How long
An hour or less. Each session is self-contained, so missing one costs you nothing.
What to bring
Curiosity and an eagerness to dive into experiments!
Who teaches it
Students. Every course is written and run by the high schoolers who built LUMEN.
Questions
Email outreach@lumen-initiative.org — we read every message.

Sign up

Signing up takes about two minutes.

We don’t keep our own waitlist. Every workshop is booked through the host library’s events calendar — the same place you’d sign up for story hour. Pick the branch nearest you and search “LUMEN”.

  1. 1

    Pick a session

    Twenty-one this August across five branches. Skim the schedule or open the full session list.

  2. 2

    Register at the library website

    Open your branch’s calendar below and reserve a spot. Rooms are small, so earlier is safer.

  3. 3

    Just show up

    We bring the strawberries, the cornstarch, the straws and the laptops. Your kid brings curiosity.
